After a while, it's finally time to update my portfolio. I'm very proud to share this project. Using a mix of 3D design and traditional techniques, we created an artistic interpretation of the Derveni Krater. The piece stands 150 cm tall and carries a strong bronze presence, staying close to the essence of the original while reimagining its scale and details.

## About the Derveni Krater

The Derveni Krater is one of the most famous treasures of ancient Greece. It was discovered in 1962 near Thessaloniki and dates back to the late 4th century BCE. The original piece is a large bronze vessel, richly decorated with figures of Dionysus, Ariadne, satyrs and maenads — symbols of wine, celebration and the mysteries of the afterlife. Today it can be seen in the Archaeological Museum of Thessaloniki.
